# Customer Analytics Project
Customer analytics consulting project — identifying high-revenue Airbnb acquisition targets using Random Forest and Decision Tree models across 250,000+ listings in 10 global cities

Airbnb Market Intelligence — Customer Analytics Project

## Overview

A semester long consulting engagement for Cobalt Capital Partners, a simulated real estate investment trust specialising in short term rentals. The objective is to identify which property characteristics and guest experience factors drive listing success across 250,000+ Airbnb listings and 5 million guest reviews in 10 global cities, and translate those findings into data backed acquisition recommendations.

## Business Problem

The short term rental market has matured. Location and size alone no longer predict which properties will outperform. This project builds an analytical framework that moves beyond traditional real estate signals to identify the hidden characteristics that drive both high revenue and strong guest demand.

## Analytical Approach

Two analytical pathways are built around a unified Success Proxy — a composite measure of nightly price and occupancy demand.
1. Pathway 1 — Property Characteristics: Random Forest Regressor to identify which physical property attributes most strongly predict the Success Proxy, followed by a Decision Tree classifier to surface the specific property profile that consistently appears among top tier listings.
2. Pathway 2 — Guest Experience: Random Forest Regressor on structured guest satisfaction scores, combined with NLP sentiment analysis and word frequency analysis on 5 million guest reviews to identify which operational factors drive listing success.
